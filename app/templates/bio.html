Hello! I'm Andrew Swartz, a Computer Science and Emerging Media student at Ithaca College pursuing dual bachelor's degrees. I enjoy combining software engineering with creativity to build websites, games, and interactive digital experiences.

My interests include full-stack web development, game development with Godot, video production, and emerging technologies. Whether I'm building a Flask application, editing educational videos, or designing a new game, I enjoy solving problems and creating engaging experiences for users.
